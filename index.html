The 529 Situation

What happens to education savings after graduation

If $1,000 Is Withdrawn Today

Non-qualified 529 withdrawals incur a 10% federal penalty plus income tax on the earnings portion. On a $1,000 withdrawal, roughly $100 goes to penalties and taxes.

Options for the $7,000

  • Leave it in the 529 Grows tax-free, but only usable for future education expenses
  • Non-qualified withdrawal Get the money out, but lose ~10% to penalties & taxes on earnings
  • Roth IRA rollover Only available if the account has been open 15+ years (SECURE 2.0 Act)
  • Student loan repayment Up to $10,000 lifetime limit per beneficiary
  • Invest in a growing business ↓ Highest potential return — the case is below
The core problem: I've graduated. The 529 was built for college, and that job is done. Every year this money sits idle, it's earning modest returns with a penalty cloud over it. Meanwhile, my businesses are compounding at 9x the rate.
